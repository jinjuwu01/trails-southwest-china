崇祯十一年四月二十三日 · 黔中白水河（今黄果树瀑布一带）The 23rd day of the 4th month, 11th year of Chongzhen (1638) · the Baishui River, central Guizhou (today’s Huangguoshu Falls area)

这一天，徐霞客从镇宁往西，经白水河铺，看见了黄果树瀑布，留下了对它最早的详细描写。替他挑行李的人，日记里只写作“担夫”。
今天，你就是那个担夫。拿他当天的日记当线索，到山水里把每一句话对上。

On this day Xu Xiake traveled west from Zhenning, passed Baishuihe Post, and saw the Huangguoshu Falls, leaving the earliest detailed description of them. The man who carried his luggage appears in the diary only as “the porter.”
Today, you are that porter. Use Xu Xiake’s diary for that day as your clues, and match each line to the hills and waters around you.

徐霞客《黔游日记》四月二十三日（节录）Xu Xiake, Guizhou Diary, the 23rd day of the 4th month (excerpts)

担夫批注Porter’s Notes

这一天的日记里，担夫开口说了两回话：一回告诉他前头还有更大的瀑布，一回告诉他前面有望水亭可以歇脚。
日记记下了这两句话，没有记下担夫的名字。

In that day’s diary the porter speaks twice: once to tell him a bigger waterfall lies ahead, and once to tell him there is a Water-Viewing Pavilion ahead where they can rest.
The diary recorded both remarks. It did not record the porter’s name.

原型说明：山水依日记所写的方位与次序示意重建（白水河铺、陇隙、白虹桥、悬捣处、望水亭），并非实测地形；“陡坡塘”“黄果树瀑布”是今名。担夫、寨子、插秧的人与全部对话为虚构。日记引文据《徐霞客游记·黔游日记一》崇祯十一年四月二十三日条，有节略；担夫第一回说的话只作转述，未引原文。

About this prototype: the landscape is a schematic reconstruction that follows the directions and sequence given in the diary (Baishuihe Post, the ridge gap, White Rainbow Bridge, the falls, the Water-Viewing Pavilion); it is not surveyed terrain. “Doupotang” and “Huangguoshu Falls” are modern names. The porter, the village, the rice planters and all of the dialogue are fictional. Diary quotations follow the entry for the 23rd day of the 4th month, 11th year of Chongzhen, in “Guizhou Diary I” of The Travels of Xu Xiake, abridged; the porter’s first remark is paraphrased, not quoted. The English translations of the diary are this project’s own.

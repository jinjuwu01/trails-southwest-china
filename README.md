# 西南行迹：明清山河与行旅世界

**Trails through Southwest China: Travel and Everyday Life in the Ming–Qing Era**

## 第一章：担夫之路 · Chapter One: The Porter's Road

**在线试玩 · Play online:** https://jinjuwu01.github.io/trails-southwest-china/

崇祯十一年四月二十三日（1638 年），徐霞客从镇宁往西，经白水河铺，看见了黄果树瀑布，留下了对它最早的详细描写。替他挑行李的人，日记里只写作“担夫”。今天，你就是那个担夫。拿他当天的日记当线索，到山水里把每一句话对上。

On the 23rd day of the 4th month, 1638, Xu Xiake travelled west from Zhenning, passed Baishuihe Post, and saw the Huangguoshu Falls, leaving the earliest detailed description of them. The man who carried his luggage appears in the diary only as "the porter." Today, you are that porter. Use Xu Xiake's diary for that day as your clues, and match each line to the hills and waters around you.

### 说明 · About this prototype

山水依日记所写的方位与次序示意重建（白水河铺、陇隙、白虹桥、悬捣处、望水亭），并非实测地形；“陡坡塘”“黄果树瀑布”是今名。担夫、寨子、插秧的人与全部对话为虚构。日记引文据《徐霞客游记·黔游日记一》，有节略；英文为本项目自译。

The landscape is a schematic reconstruction that follows the directions and sequence given in the diary; it is not surveyed terrain. The porter, the village, the people planting rice and all dialogue are fictional. Diary quotations follow *The Travels of Xu Xiake*, "Guizhou Diary I," abridged; the English translations are this project's own.
